# GuildFlow – Your Guild. Your Adventure.

**GuildFlow** is a guild management addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. It brings members, events, raids, loot, professions and shared goals together in one in-game interface.

Whether you organize raids or simply want to find guildmates, GuildFlow helps you see what's happening in your guild and take part.

## Guild Overview

The redesigned dashboard puts the most useful information in one place: guild members and roles, upcoming events, recent activity, guild goals and suggestions for what to do next.

New guild members can find useful contacts, activities and a starting point without having to search through chat messages.

## Members, Equipment and Talents

Browse guild members by class and role, see their online status, and open detailed profiles with main/alt information, professions and talent builds.

The in-game equipment view shows gear slots, item information and a rotatable 3D character model where supported. Your own character and eligible nearby members can be displayed using a live model. For distant characters, an approximate appearance may be shown if enough information is available.

Talent trees show spent points directly on the talent icons, making builds easier to inspect.

## Calendar, Raids and Loot

**Guild Calendar & Events**

Plan raids, dungeons and other guild activities using a shared calendar. Create recurring events, collect sign-ups, assign roles, manage waitlists and use reminders.

**Raid Management**

Prepare raid groups, check class buffs, follow attendance and boss progress, and review raid statistics.

**Loot Management**

Manage Soft Reserves, priorities, Loot Council decisions, wishlists and loot history. Optional AtlasLoot integration makes selecting items easier.

## Professions and Guild Crafting Orders

GuildFlow shares profession and recipe information between participating guild members, so you can search for an item and see who in your guild can make it.

Create a crafting order for a specific recipe, set the quantity and decide who supplies the materials. Eligible crafters can offer to take the job. After assignment, an order can be tracked through crafting and delivery.

GuildFlow highlights relevant open crafting orders without intrusive popups or notification sounds. Linked and mutually verified alt characters can also contribute known recipes, while the addon keeps track of which character actually knows them.

### Personal Shopping List

Plan materials for items you want to craft yourself or have crafted by guildmates.

- Keep multiple recipes and quantities in one private list.
- Mark planned items as **Craft myself** or **No order**.
- Create a guild order only for the recipe you explicitly select.
- When you create a guild order and choose to provide the materials yourself, its required materials are included in your shopping plan.
- Combine material requirements across recipes without counting an existing planned quantity twice.
- Compare what you need with what is currently in your bags.

The shopping list stays separate from guild orders. Selecting a recipe or adding it to your list never publishes a guild order by itself. Material availability depends on the recipe information provided by the WoW client; guild-bank stock is not included in bag totals.

### Craftable Equipment Upgrades

GuildFlow can suggest armor and jewelry upgrades that guildmates may be able to craft for your **currently logged-in character**. Inspect item tooltips, check available crafters and review materials before creating an order.

These are suggestions based on item level and basic equipment compatibility, not guaranteed best-in-slot recommendations. Coverage of weapons and enchantments is limited.

## Group Finder, Projects and Statistics

Find guildmates for quests, dungeons and other activities. Organize shared guild projects, follow member progression, review activity and raid statistics, and contact guild leadership through private tickets.

## GuildFlow Sync – Optional Windows Companion

**GuildFlow Sync is optional. The WoW addon works on its own.**

The Windows companion adds features for guilds that want to exchange supported data between gaming sessions, including when members are not online at the same time.

**Background Synchronization**

With a guild-configured Discord connection, GuildFlow Sync can exchange supported guild information in the background, including events, registrations and other updates.

**Windows Armory**

View the equipment last recorded for guild members in a dedicated desktop interface. The Windows Armory includes an equipped 3D character model with rotation, zoom and pose controls, alongside item slots and gear information.

**Advanced Statistics**

Explore guild activity, membership, attendance, raid progression, loot and other statistics using charts and detailed views.

**Installation and Updates**

GuildFlow Sync is available as a Windows installer and a portable build. It includes update management for the Windows companion and addon.

**Compatibility:** Windows 10 and 11. Find GuildFlow Sync and its installation instructions through the official GitHub link in this project's Links section.

## Getting Started

Install GuildFlow through CurseForge, start World of Warcraft: Forever and open the addon with **/gf** or through the GuildFlow tab in the guild window.

Enter **/gf demo** to explore the interface with simulated guild data.

GuildFlow is designed for **WoW Forever 1.60.1** and offers **12 interface languages**. Some features require other participating guild members to share supported information; not every character or recipe can provide complete equipment or material data.
