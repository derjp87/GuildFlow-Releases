# GuildFlow – Your Guild. Your Progress.

GuildFlow is a guild management and social coordination addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever (1.60.1)**. Organize raids, find guildmates, share professions, coordinate crafting and track guild progress directly inside WoW.

## What's inside the addon

**Redesigned guild dashboard:** See your guild's members and roles, upcoming events, recent activity, useful next steps and shared goals at a glance.

**Guild members, talents and equipment:** Browse the roster, roles, professions, mains and alts. Member profiles show equipment and talent builds. The in-game Armory shows equipment slots and a rotatable 3D character model where supported. Live models can be shown for your own character and certain nearby members; distant members may use an approximation when the available data permits.

**Raids, events and loot:** Plan raids and other guild events through the calendar, recurring events and role-based sign-ups. Organize raid groups and manage attendance, boss progression, Soft Reserves, Loot Council, wishlists and loot history.

**Professions and crafting orders:** Discover which guild members can craft an item, review its materials and contact a crafter. Create individual guild crafting orders specifying quantity and who provides the ingredients. A private shopping list combines materials for recipes you craft yourself and guild orders for which you supply ingredients. Recipes can be marked as "Craft myself" or "No order"; choosing a recipe does not automatically create an order.

**Group finding and guild activities:** Find guildmates for dungeons, quests and activities. Guild start, projects, activity updates, personal progress, statistics and guild support tools help new and experienced members coordinate.

## Optional GuildFlow Sync for Windows

GuildFlow Sync is a free optional companion application for Windows. The addon works without it.

The companion can exchange supported guild data between gaming sessions through a guild-controlled Discord setup and provides detailed guild statistics, automatic background sync and an extended Windows Armory with an equipped 3D character model, poses and zoom controls.

Available as an installer or a portable build from the [official GitHub releases](https://github.com/derjp87/GuildFlow-Releases/releases).

## Getting started

Install GuildFlow, enable the three included addon folders and open it with `/gf` or the GuildFlow tab in the guild window.

Try `/gf demo` to explore the interface with simulated guild data. GuildFlow Sync is only needed if your guild wants the additional offline features.

GuildFlow supports 12 interface languages. Some remote equipment views, character models and recipe details depend on what the WoW client and other guild members have shared.
